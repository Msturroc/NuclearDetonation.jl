Second Tracer Release

The second tracer release started on 14 November, 15:00 UTC and 
ended on 15 November, 2:45 UTC. During these 12 hours a total of 
490 kg of PMCP were emitted, resulting in an average release rate 
of 11.56 g/s. The air stream (71 m3/h) at the top of the chimney 
had an average temperature of 73 degC and a velocity of about 50 m/s.
